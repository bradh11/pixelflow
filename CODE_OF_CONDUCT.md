# Code of conduct

PixelFlow is a hobby project about making light shows. People come to it with very different
amounts of experience, and everyone who takes part in its issues, pull requests, and discussions
is expected to help keep it a good place to be.

## What we ask

- Be kind and patient. Assume the other person means well and may be new to this.
- Talk about the work: the bug, the idea, the code. Not the person.
- Disagree plainly and politely, and accept that the maintainer makes the final call on what is
  merged.
- Respect people's time. A clear report or a small, focused change is a courtesy.
- Keep other people's private details private, and take personal details out of anything you
  share: show files, logs, screenshots.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments, about anyone and for any reason, including who
  they are, where they are from, or how much they know.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, and promotion that has nothing to do with the project.

## If something goes wrong

Tell the maintainer privately, through the contact options on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/bradh11), or use GitHub's own **Report
content** option on the comment or issue. Reports are kept in confidence.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock a thread, and block from the project
anyone who keeps behaving in a way this page rules out.

This applies in the project's spaces on GitHub, and anywhere someone is speaking for the
project.
