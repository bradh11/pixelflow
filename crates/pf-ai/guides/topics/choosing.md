# Choosing effects

Every effect on one page, to choose between them before reading the guides of only the ones you will place.

## How to choose

- Start from the prop's role (topic `roles`): an outline or an arch wants something that travels along it, a matrix a picture, a tree something that wraps or climbs it, the whole house a wash or a sweep.
- Then from the music (topic `intensity`): quiet parts want a steady `on`, a slow `twinkle` or `colorWash`; loud ones movement and more props; the biggest moments a burst such as `impact`, `shockwave`, or `fireworks`.
- Then give each canvas (a matrix, a tree) a showpiece somewhere in the song, and rotate effects across the different parts rather than repeating one kit: topic `showpieces` says where each showpiece belongs and which effects suit which props.
- In brackets after each effect, the kinds of prop its ready-made looks were written for: one with no look for your prop may still work, but read its guide first.
- Then read the guides of the effects you chose, all in one `get_guide` call. With `parts` an effect's guide comes in pieces: `looks` for its ready-made looks alone, `settings` and `reference` for its settings, `draws` for what it does on each kind of prop, `use` for when to use it. The first time you use an effect, read its `draws` and `looks` at least: what it draws on a string is rarely what it draws on a matrix.
