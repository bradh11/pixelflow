# The range

What each effect can do, in a line or two: the moments and moods it suits and the props it works on, so the whole palette is in view when choosing. Nothing here is a rule.

## Choosing from it

Every effect below has a full guide (`get_guide` with `effects`) with what it draws on each kind of prop and its ready-made looks; the look named on each line is one place to start. Any of them can serve a show's idea: a familiar formula is fine, and so is something nobody expects. The props in brackets are the ones its looks were written for. Effects that need columns and rows say so: on a single run of pixels (a roofline, an arch) they show only a slice of themselves, and `review_draft` points that out. These are:

## Light and color

- `on` (string, arch, matrix, tree, house): a steady color, the floor under a look, a swell or a fade over a held chord. "Warm white hold".
- `off` (string, arch, matrix, tree, house): dark on purpose: a prop resting through a verse, a dip before a hit. "Dip to black".
- `colorWash` (string, arch, matrix, tree, house): a slow drift between neighbouring colors, a calm base for a section with movement above it. "Slow drift from deep blue to violet".
- `colorShift` (string, arch, matrix, tree, house): a change of color that travels or lands at once: a key change, a new section, a drop. "New color crossing the display".
- `fade` (string, arch, matrix, tree, house): a flash that dies away or a swell into a downbeat. "White hit that dies away".
- `pulse` (string, arch, matrix, tree, house): breathing on the beat or the bass, the base of a rhythmic section. "Slow breath on the bars".
- `twinkle` (string, arch, matrix, tree, house): starlight, sparse and slow for an intro or a bridge, thick for a glittering chorus. "Starlight".
- `shimmer` (string, arch, matrix, tree, house): a whole prop blinking between colors on the beat; keep it slow on anything large (topic `safety`). "Blink on the beat, red then green".
- `strobe` (string, arch, matrix, tree, house): scattered flashes, a still field of stars or a burst at the very biggest hit. "Still field of stars".

## Movement along and across

- `chase` (string, arch, matrix, tree, house): bands running along a line, a marquee round a roofline, light passing from arch to arch. "A white runner along an outline".
- `bars` (string, arch, matrix, tree, house): stripes sliding along or rising up, the steady motion of an up-tempo section. "Candy stripe along an outline".
- `wave` (string, arch, matrix, tree, house): a swinging ribbon or a swell of water, gentle or brisk. "A ribbon round the tree".
- `spiral` (string, arch, matrix, tree, house): stripes wrapping and turning, the classic moving tree. "Candy cane tree".
- `wipe` (string, arch, matrix, tree, house): a new color sweeping across, a section change, a reveal. "New color sweeping in".
- `fill` (string, arch, matrix, tree, house): a prop filling up, a build from the ground, a level meter. "Filling from the ground up".
- `garlands` (string, arch, matrix, tree, house): lines stacking until a prop is full, a build or a decorated tree. "A tree filling from the foot".
- `morph` (string, arch, matrix, tree, house): a shape of light travelling from one place to another: a comet, a curtain, a hit with a direction. "A comet from left to right".
- `pinwheel` (string, arch, matrix, tree, house): arms turning round a center, a running look for a chorus on a matrix or across the house. "Peppermint swirl".
- `fan` (matrix, house): blades that open, turn, and fly apart: a flourish with a beginning and an end, or a burst on a hit. "Candy propeller".
- `ripple` (string, arch, matrix, tree, house): rings spreading endlessly from the middle: water, a held note, the house breathing. "Water rings".

## Bursts and accents

- `impact` (string, arch, matrix, tree, house): a hit that lands and dies away, in white or the section's color. "White hit with a long tail".
- `shockwave` (string, arch, matrix, tree, house): one ring bursting out (or closing in) with its effect: a hit, or a wind-up into one. "Blast on a hit".
- `fireworks` (string, arch, matrix, tree, house): bursts of sparks, one huge or many overlapping: a finale, an accent, a celebration. "A firework finale on a matrix".
- `lightning` (string, arch, matrix, tree): bolts and flickers, a storm or a spooky crackle; mind the flashing (topic `safety`). "Storm on a matrix".

## Pictures and textures

- `plasma` (string, arch, matrix, tree, house): soft clouds of color folding slowly, a living background with no beat. "Icy clouds".
- `shader` (string, arch, matrix, tree, house): rich moving pictures from PixelFlow's shaders, from northern lights to a kaleidoscope. "Northern lights on the matrix".
- `butterfly` (string, arch, matrix, tree, house): swirling color patterns, from a busy rainbow to frost. "Frost wings".
- `circles` (string, arch, matrix, tree, house): balls and rings: ornaments, bubbles, a lava lamp, a bullseye. "Drifting ornaments".
- `lines` (string, arch, matrix, tree): moving lines and shapes, laser sticks, a texture for an instrumental. "Laser sticks".
- `life` (matrix, tree, house): cells living and dying, quirky, electronic, or slimy. "Bubbling slime".
- `tendril` (string, arch, matrix, tree, house): a living, hand-drawn line wandering or circling, warm or ghostly. "A gold ribbon circling a matrix".
- `fire` (string, arch, matrix, tree, house): flames, from a low hearth to the whole house burning. "A low hearth fire".
- `meteors` (string, arch, matrix, tree, house): streaks falling or rising: rain, snow, sparks, a comet along a roofline. "Icy rain".
- `snowflakes` (string, arch, matrix, tree, house): falling snow, drifting flakes, a drift piling up at the end. "Gentle snowfall".
- `shape` (string, arch, matrix, tree, house): stars, hearts, snowflakes, and presents popping or drifting. "Gold stars popping with the music".
- `dancer` (matrix): a dancing character, the matrix's act for a chorus or a break. "A ghost in icy blue".
- `picture` (matrix): one of the show's own pictures or GIFs, held, moving, or growing. "A picture held on a matrix".

## Words, singing, and the music itself

- `text` (matrix, tree): words: a greeting, a title, a word landed on a downbeat, a countdown. "A word held on a matrix".
- `faces` (matrix, house): a face singing the words, on a prop that has one. "A face singing the lead".
- `sing` (string, arch, matrix, tree, house): any prop moving with the words, pulsing or filling as they are sung. "An outline that sings".
- `vuMeter` (string, arch, matrix, tree, house): the music made visible: meters, spectrums, sweeps. "A spectrum analyser on a matrix".

Topic `choosing` sets them side by side again with each guide's own summary; topic `roles` says what each kind of prop is for in a show.
